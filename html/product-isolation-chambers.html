Isolation Chambers

Isolation Chambers

Containment Solutions for Critical Cleanroom Operations

Designed to provide controlled containment during transfers, sampling and quarantine. Available in negative/positive pressure configurations with validated airflow and filtration.

Key Features

Engineered to maintain pressure integrity and contamination control across every handling scenario.

  • Negative and positive pressure modes with magnehelic monitoring
  • HEPA H14 supply & exhaust filtration with leak-tested housings
  • Electromagnetic interlock with illuminated door status indicators
  • 304 SS interior, flush glazing and radius covings for effortless cleaning
  • Integrated UV germicidal lamp and hour meter (optional)
  • Seamless integration with BMS / alarm systems for remote monitoring

Containment Advantages

Built to sustain validated pressure differentials and critical compliance standards.

  • Real-time pressure status with audible & visual alerts
  • LAM flow certification and leak-tested filter assemblies
  • Ergonomic access for operators and material transfer

Applications

Reliable containment for regulated processing and material control.

  • API and sterile formulation facilities
  • Sampling, dispensing and quarantine holding
  • Pathology, microbiology and hospital pharmacies
