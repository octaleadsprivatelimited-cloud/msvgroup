Sterile Packaging Line – Visakhapatnam

ISO 7 Lyophilised Product Packaging & Dispatch

MSV Group modernised a sterile packaging suite to streamline lyophilised product handling, integrate serialisation workflows and ensure end-to-end cold-chain compliance.

Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh 9-week program 6,200 sq.ft upgrade

Scope Delivered

Packaging efficiency uplift with compliant cleanroom envelope and utilities.

  • ISO 7 packaging core, ISO 8 support rooms and segregated dispatch corridor
  • High-gloss ESD epoxy floors with slope to drain and cleanable covings
  • Integrated serialisation, labelling and inspection conveyors
  • Temperature-controlled staging with data-logged cold storage
  • Material airlocks with SS pass-boxes and UV decontamination

Key Systems Installed

Utilities and controls supporting rapid, compliant packaging operations.

  • HVAC with HEPA supply and return air monitoring for packaging lanes
  • Barcode serialisation platform linked to ERP for batch traceability
  • Energy-efficient LED lighting with glare control for inspection stations
  • Fire detection and VESDA integration through packaging and dispatch areas

Project Outcomes

Improved throughput with stringent cold-chain adherence.

  • 15% faster packaging cycles with serialisation automation
  • Zero deviations recorded during post-upgrade regulatory audits
  • Enhanced product integrity through monitored cold-chain transitions

Support & Training

Operational readiness for inspection and maintenance cycles.

  • Packaging SOPs and deviation management workflows documented
  • Staff training on cold-chain monitoring and cleanroom discipline
  • Quarterly service plan covering HVAC tuning and integrity tests

MSV Group orchestrates compliant cleanroom upgrades that keep products on schedule.
