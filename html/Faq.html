FAQ'S

Frequently Asked Questions?

Find answers to common questions about MSV Group's steel products and services

What is your production capacity?

MSV Group India operates state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities spanning over 10 lakhs square feet in Hyderabad. Our production capacity includes 50,000 steel doors and 80,000 steel windows per year. We also manufacture cleanroom equipment with advanced machinery from global suppliers.

How many wooden finishes are available for steel doors?

We offer 60 unique wooden-finish shades for residential steel doors. These finishes combine the security and durability of steel with the warmth and aesthetic appeal of natural wood, giving you extensive design options to match your home's interior and exterior décor.

What types of steel windows do you manufacture?

We manufacture three main types of steel windows: Casement Windows (traditional opening style), Sliding Windows (space-saving design), and Fixed Windows (maximum light penetration). All our windows are durable, stylish, and energy-efficient, suitable for both residential and commercial applications.

Are your products ISO certified?

Yes, MSV Group India is ISO 9001:2015 certified. Our manufacturing facilities follow stringent quality management standards, ensuring that every product meets international quality benchmarks. This certification demonstrates our commitment to consistent quality and customer satisfaction.

Do you supply products to government institutions?

Yes, we have successfully supplied school furniture including dual desks and chalkboards to government schools across Andhra Pradesh. We also provide RO (Reverse Osmosis) water purification systems to educational institutions, ensuring safe drinking water for students.

Which industries does MSV Group India serve?

We serve pharmaceutical, biotech, vaccine, hospitals, diagnostics, nutraceuticals, micro-electronics, packaging and other regulated industries that require controlled environments. Our team understands cGMP, NABH and other compliance needs and designs projects accordingly.

Can you handle turnkey projects from design to validation?

Yes. MSV Group offers end-to-end solutions including layout design, HVAC and cleanroom engineering, paneling, doors, AHUs, pass boxes, LAFs, HEPA filtration, utilities coordination and support during qualification/validation.

Do you manufacture products in-house?

Most key cleanroom elements are manufactured in-house at our Hyderabad facility � including PUF panels, metal doors, pass boxes, laminar air flow units, dust collectors, and HEPA & pre-filters � giving us better quality control and faster delivery.

What information do you need to start a cleanroom proposal?

Typically we request basic layout drawings, process flow, required cleanliness class, regulatory standards to be followed, desired utilities and approximate timelines. Our team can also visit your site to study constraints and suggest an optimized concept.

Do you provide after-sales service and maintenance?

Yes, we provide comprehensive after-sales support including AMC options, replacement filters, equipment servicing and on-site troubleshooting to keep your cleanroom and equipment performing reliably.
