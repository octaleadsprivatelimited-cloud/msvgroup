FAQ'S

Frequently Asked Questions?

Find answers to common questions about MSV Group's steel products and services

What is your production capacity?

MSV Group India operates state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities spanning over 10 lakhs square feet in Hyderabad. Our production capacity includes 50,000 steel doors and 80,000 steel windows per year. We also manufacture cleanroom equipment with advanced machinery from global suppliers.

How many wooden finishes are available for steel doors?

We offer 60 unique wooden-finish shades for residential steel doors. These finishes combine the security and durability of steel with the warmth and aesthetic appeal of natural wood, giving you extensive design options to match your home's interior and exterior dÃ©cor.

What types of steel windows do you manufacture?

We manufacture three main types of steel windows: Casement Windows (traditional opening style), Sliding Windows (space-saving design), and Fixed Windows (maximum light penetration). All our windows are durable, stylish, and energy-efficient, suitable for both residential and commercial applications.

Are your products ISO certified?

Yes, MSV Group India is ISO 9001:2015 certified. Our manufacturing facilities follow stringent quality management standards, ensuring that every product meets international quality benchmarks. This certification demonstrates our commitment to consistent quality and customer satisfaction.

Do you supply products to government institutions?

Yes, we have successfully supplied school furniture including dual desks and chalkboards to government schools across Andhra Pradesh. We also provide RO (Reverse Osmosis) water purification systems to educational institutions, ensuring safe drinking water for students.
