GMP-Ready Nutraceutical Manufacturing Cleanroom

MSV Group built a flexible cleanroom platform for a nutraceutical manufacturer, integrating high-throughput blending, encapsulation and packaging zones with hygienic material flow.

Ahmedabad, Gujarat 11-week program 9,000 sq.ft facility

Scope Delivered

Full cleanroom envelope, utilities and process integration tailored for nutraceuticals.

  • ISO 7 processing halls with ISO 8 support corridors and staged gowning
  • Modular partitions, flush glazing and hygienic covings for powder control
  • Encapsulation, tablet compression and blending line integration
  • Dust extraction network with high-efficiency collectors and HEPA polish
  • Finished goods packaging and warehousing with segregated flows

Key Systems Installed

Reliable utilities and controls for powder processing hygiene.

  • HVAC with EC fans, terminal HEPA, humidity control and differential monitoring
  • Integrated dust collection and pneumatic conveying for powder handling
  • RO/DI water plant and stainless utility piping for process feeds
  • SCADA-linked BMS for environmental and equipment performance tracking

Project Outcomes

Faster product launches with compliant manufacturing backbone.

  • Product changeover time reduced by 25% via streamlined material flow
  • Environmental data trending simplified batch release and QA reporting
  • Facility qualified for export compliance with third-party audit support

Support & Training

Empowering client teams for sustained GMP performance.

  • Process SOP documentation covering cleaning, batch changeover and maintenance
  • Cleanroom behaviour and dust control workshops for production teams
  • Annual service plan for HVAC tuning, filter integrity and calibration
