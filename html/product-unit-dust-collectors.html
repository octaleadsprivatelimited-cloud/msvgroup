Unit Dust Collectors

Portable Dust Collection Systems

Compact, self-contained dust collectors for point-source capture of particulates, fumes and airborne contaminants in manufacturing and processing environments.

Key Features

  • Cyclone separator with high-efficiency bag/cartridge filters
  • Portable design with casters for mobility
  • Auto-pulse cleaning system for continuous operation
  • CFM range: 500 to 5000+ (custom capacities)
  • SS304/GI construction with powder-coated finish
  • Low noise operation with silencer options

Applications

  • Pharmaceutical powder handling and milling
  • Welding and grinding operations
  • Woodworking and CNC machining
  • Chemical processing and material handling

Specifications

  • Airflow: 500 to 5000+ CFM
  • Filter: HEPA/HEPA + Carbon (optional)
  • Motor: 1 HP to 15 HP (as per CFM)
  • Collection efficiency: 99.9%+
  • Noise: < 75 dB(A) with silencer
