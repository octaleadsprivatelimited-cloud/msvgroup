Negative Pressure Isolation Ward Retrofit

MSV Group converted an existing inpatient wing into an isolation ward with negative pressure rooms, integrated monitoring and biosafety protocols to treat airborne infections safely.

Mumbai, Maharashtra 6-week program 3,800 sq.ft retrofit

Scope Delivered

Patient-safe isolation environment engineered for rapid deployment.

  • Six negative pressure isolation rooms with ante rooms and interlocked doors
  • Seamless wall cladding, flush glazing and antimicrobial surfaces
  • Dedicated exhaust system with HEPA filtration and emergency bypass
  • Central nurse station with patient monitoring and alarm dashboards
  • Donning/doffing rooms, biohazard staging and waste management pathway

Key Systems Installed

Robust HVAC, controls and life safety for isolation operations.

  • PLC-based pressure monitoring with visual dashboards and alerts
  • Hands-free door hardware and antimicrobial coatings for high-touch zones
  • Medical gas integration with bed head panels and nurse call
  • Decontamination shower and UV sterilisation for reusable equipment

Project Outcomes

Operational resilience and improved patient safety metrics.

  • Zero cross-contamination cases reported post commissioning
  • Integrated monitoring reduced alarm response time to under 2 minutes
  • Rapid retrofit completed without disrupting adjacent wards

Support & Training

Maintaining biosafety readiness through robust procedures.

  • Staff training on donning/doffing, isolation protocols and waste handling
  • Preventive maintenance schedule for HVAC, alarms and filtration
  • Quarterly audits and validation support for hospital infection control
