Biosafety Containment Suite – Bengaluru

BSL-3 Readiness Containment & Quarantine Block

MSV Group partnered with a biotechnology major to retrofit an existing facility into a containment-ready suite supporting pathogen research, high-risk sample handling and validated decontamination procedures.

Bengaluru, Karnataka 8-week program 4,600 sq.ft retrofit

Scope Delivered

Containment envelope, pressure regime and decon systems engineered for resilience.

  • Negative pressure labs with interlocked pass-through hatches and magnehelic display
  • Fully welded stainless-steel interiors, radius covings and flush glazing
  • Decontamination airlocks with chemical shower integration
  • Double-door autoclave and biological waste staging room
  • Emergency backup power, alarm network and incident response signage

Key Systems Installed

Specialised HVAC and control architecture ensuring safe containment integrity.

  • Dedicated supply/exhaust AHUs with bag-in bag-out HEPA housings
  • PLC-based pressure cascade control with failsafe override
  • Continuous gas monitoring with alarm to security control room
  • Hands-free antimicrobial doors, bump rails and cleanable lighting systems

Project Outcomes

Enhanced biosafety posture with rapid regulatory approvals.

  • Achieved BSL-3 readiness certification with third-party validation
  • Zero containment breaches in first 6 months of operation
  • Automated reports simplified audit documentation for CDSCO & WHO

Support & Training

Ensuring operational readiness and emergency preparedness.

  • Containment drills covering pressure excursions and power failures
  • Maintenance manuals for bag-in bag-out HEPA change procedures
  • Annual service contract with quarterly integrity and airflow tests
