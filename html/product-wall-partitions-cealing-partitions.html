Wall Partitions & Cealing Partitions

Wall & Ceiling Systems

Modular Cleanroom Wall & Ceiling Partition Systems

Pre-engineered modular panel systems for cleanroom construction with seamless joints, superior insulation and ISO-compliant air tightness.

Key Features

Engineered for seamless integration, strict environmental control and quick deployment inside critical facilities.

  • PUF insulated panels with GI or SS304 outer skins for durability
  • Cam-lock or tongue-and-groove joining system ensuring airtight seals
  • Available in 50mm, 75mm and 100mm thickness profiles
  • Cleanroom-grade surface finish with flush windows and door integrations
  • Fire-rated configurations (Standard and Class A/FRP) for compliance
  • Modular design enables rapid installation and effortless reconfiguration

Performance Edge

Precision-built envelopes that protect processes and streamline maintenance.

  • Seamless junctions that maintain ISO-classified airflow paths
  • Validated airtightness supporting pressure cascade requirements
  • On-site assembly teams for minimal shutdown and faster validation
Discuss Your Project

Applications

Adaptable modular envelopes for regulated production and research environments.

  • Pharmaceutical manufacturing facilities
  • Biotechnology and research laboratories
  • Hospital OT, ICU and isolation rooms
  • Electronics manufacturing cleanrooms
  • Food processing and hygienic packaging units

Technical Snapshot

Configurable panel assemblies built to meet ISO, GMP and FDA audit expectations.

Panel Thickness 50mm · 75mm · 100mm
Insulation Core PUF · 40-45 kg/m³ density
Skin Material Powder-coated GI or SS304
Thermal Performance U-value 0.23 - 0.28 W/m²K
Fire Rating Standard & Class A (FRP) options
