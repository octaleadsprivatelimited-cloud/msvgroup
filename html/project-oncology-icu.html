Oncology ICU – Hyderabad

12-Bed Oncology ICU & ISO Class 5 OT Complex

MSV Group delivered a turnkey critical-care upgrade for a leading oncology hospital in Hyderabad, integrating ISO-compliant operating suites, pressurised isolation rooms and fully networked nurse stations—all completed without interrupting day-to-day clinical services.

Hyderabad, Telangana 14-week program 12,000 sq.ft delivery

Scope Delivered

Integrated civil, MEP and clinical infrastructure for a high-acuity oncology unit.

  • Reconfigured patient & sterile corridors with hermetically sealed ICU pods
  • ISO Class 5 operating theaters with laminar diffusers and terminal HEPA filtration
  • Pressurised isolation rooms & anteroom cascade for immunocompromised patients
  • Centralised medical gas manifold with redundant supply and nurse call integration
  • Custom bed head panels, pendant boom integration and ceiling service raceways

Key Systems Installed

Best-in-class HVAC, medical gas and digital backbone for precision monitoring.

  • Variable air volume AHUs with pressure cascade automation & alarms
  • Antimicrobial vinyl flooring & coved skirting for infection control
  • Touch-screen surgeon panels, PACS displays & low-voltage integration
  • Nurse call with zoned annunciation linked to isolation room statuses

Project Outcomes

Tangible improvements in infection control and caregiver response time.

  • 30% reduction in turnaround time between surgical procedures
  • Continuous pressure & HVAC analytics streamed to central command
  • Intuitive nurse interfaces decreased response time by 18%
  • Turnkey validation (IQ/OQ/PQ) met NABH & ISO 14644 audit benchmarks

Support & Validation

Structured documentation and training to keep the facility compliant.

  • Comprehensive SOP handover & preventive maintenance schedules
  • Staff orientation on isolation protocols, HVAC dashboards & gas safety
  • Annual support plan covering calibration, filter integrity testing & AHU IQ
