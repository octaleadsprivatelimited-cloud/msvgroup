Pass Boxes

Material Transfer

Static & Dynamic Pass Boxes

Engineered pass boxes for safe, low-turbulence material transfer between classified areas, minimising cross-contamination risk.

Key Features

Robust construction and airflow control aligned with cleanroom norms.

  • 304/316 SS double-skin construction with flush interiors
  • Electromagnetic or mechanical door interlocks
  • Static and dynamic variants with HEPA filtration
  • Pressure gauges and indicator lights for status monitoring
  • Smooth, easy-to-clean inner surfaces and radiused corners
  • Custom sizes and floor / wall-mounted configurations

Project Capabilities

Tailored pass box solutions for regulated environments.

  • Static, dynamic and air-shower pass boxes
  • Integration with existing BMS/EMS
  • On-site installation and sealing to wall systems
  • IQ/OQ/PQ documentation and testing support
Enquire about Pass Boxes

Applications

Designed for high-containment and sterile transfer use cases.

  • Pharma formulation & sterile manufacturing
  • Biotech and vaccine production facilities
  • Hospital OTs, ICUs and CSSD corridors
  • Electronics, microelectronics and assembly cleanrooms

Why MSV Pass Boxes?

Built and validated by a team focused on long-term compliance.

  • In-house design, fabrication and installation
  • Compatibility with MSV doors, panels and HVAC
  • Support for leak testing and airflow balancing
  • Service, spares and upgrade pathways
