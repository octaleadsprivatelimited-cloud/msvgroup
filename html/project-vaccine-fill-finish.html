Vaccine Fill-Finish Facility – Pune

ISO Class 6 Vaccine Fill & Finish Suite

To support accelerated vaccine rollouts, MSV Group designed and delivered a cGMP-compliant fill-finish facility with modular cleanrooms, automated material flow and fully instrumented HVAC/BMS infrastructure.

Pune, Maharashtra 10-week program 8,500 sq.ft delivery

Scope Delivered

Turnkey engineering, fabrication and qualification for high-throughput fill suites.

  • ISO Class 6 core with ISO 7 support corridors and cascading gowning sequence
  • Modular PUF partitions, flush vision panels and solvent-safe epoxy flooring
  • Automated vial washing, filling and stoppering line integration
  • Material & personnel airlocks with RFID access and differential pressure monitoring
  • Cold-chain ante-rooms, QC labs and packaging zone segregation

Key Systems Installed

Fully validated utilities supporting aseptic processing and data integrity.

  • EC fan coil HVAC with terminal HEPA, VFD control and particle monitoring
  • CIP/SIP-ready process piping with condensate recovery
  • BMS integration for temperature, humidity and differential pressure logging
  • cGMP-compliant data historian with audit trails and alarm management

Project Outcomes

Higher batch throughput with complete regulatory traceability.

  • Increased production capacity to 6 million vials per month
  • Real-time cleanroom analytics reduced deviation response time by 22%
  • Successful WFI/utility qualification and media fill campaign on first attempt
  • Complete validation dossier furnished for WHO GMP and ISO inspections

Support & Training

Documentation and knowledge transfer to maintain aseptic reliability.

  • Operator training on gowning, airflow discipline and aseptic interventions
  • Preventive maintenance program for HVAC, HEPA integrity and utilities
  • Annual support agreement covering calibration and environmental qualification

Partner with MSV Group to design, manufacture and validate cleanroom infrastructure that meets global vaccine standards.
