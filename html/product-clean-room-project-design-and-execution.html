Clean Room Project Design & Execution

Turnkey Clean Room Project Design & Execution

End-to-end clean room design, supply and execution with ISO-compliant envelope, HVAC, filtration, validation documentation and on-site support.

Scope of Services

Comprehensive project support from the first sketch to ongoing upkeep.

  • Concept design and detailed engineering
  • ISO Class 5 to Class 8 modular cleanroom construction
  • HVAC system design, supply and installation
  • HEPA filtration integration and validation
  • Electrical, lighting and controls
  • Fire safety systems and door interlocks
  • IQ/OQ/PQ documentation and validation support
  • Post-commissioning maintenance and training

Project Capabilities

Proven execution depth for regulated environments.

  • Turnkey projects up to 10 Lakh+ sft.
  • ISO 14644 compliant methodologies
  • 15+ years of multi-industry experience
  • Deployments across 10+ Indian cities
  • GMP/WHO compliant documentation packs
Request Consultation

Applications

High-performance spaces tailored to stringent process needs.

  • Pharmaceutical manufacturing (API, formulation, sterile)
  • Biotechnology and vaccine facilities
  • Medical device manufacturing
  • Electronics and semiconductor cleanrooms
  • Hospital OT/ICU and pathology labs

Why MSV Group?

Dedicated, responsive partnerships that deliver measurable compliance.

  • Integrated design, manufacturing and installation teams
  • Robust vendor ecosystem for critical cleanroom components
  • Validation-ready documentation aligned to global audits
  • Rapid deployment with dedicated on-site supervision
  • Post-handover support, training and AMC programs
