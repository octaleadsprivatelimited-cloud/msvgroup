Hepa Filters, Pre & Fine Filters

Filtration Systems

High-Efficiency Particulate Air Filters & Pre-Filters

Manufactured in Class 10,000 cleanroom conditions. HEPA H13/H14 filters, pre-filters and fine filters for HVAC systems, LAF units, cleanroom applications and dust collection systems. ISO 14644 compliant with DOP/PAO leak testing.

HEPA Filters

Precision-built H13/H14 modules that safeguard critical environments.

  • H13 (99.95% @ 0.3 µm) and H14 (99.995% @ 0.3 µm) efficiency
  • EN1822 classified and DOP/PAO leak tested
  • Pleated media with aluminium separators
  • Custom sizes from 2ft x 2ft up to 4ft x 4ft and beyond
  • Frame materials: Aluminium, SS304 or GI
  • Sealing options: Neoprene, polyurethane or silicone gaskets

Certified Assurance

Traceable documentation and testing for every batch.

  • 100% leak testing with DOP/PAO scans
  • ISO 14644 compliant processes
  • Complete GMP documentation packs

Pre-Filters & Fine Filters

Staged filtration solutions that extend HEPA life and improve airflow efficiency.

  • G4, F5, F7, F9 efficiency grades
  • Pocket, panel and bag filter configurations
  • Extended service life with washable options
  • Compatible with standard HVAC housings
