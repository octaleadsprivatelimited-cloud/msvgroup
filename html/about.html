About MSV YOUNG ENTREPRENEURS INDIA PVT. LTD.

Cleanroom Project Design & Equipment Manufacturing

MSV YOUNG ENTREPRENEURS INDIA PVT. LTD. was incorporated to serve the Cleanroom requirements in Project design and supply of Equipment's. We are equipped with modern CNC machines, Fibre Laser Cutting machines, High pressure foaming machines to manufacture PUF panels, Cleanroom metal doors, AHU's, Pass Boxes, LAFs, Unit Dust collectors, Hepa, Pre & Filters. We also have a cleanroom built to make Hepa Filters under Class 10000 conditions.
