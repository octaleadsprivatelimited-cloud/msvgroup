About MSV Group India

About Us

MSV Group India is a customized clean room solutions provider. Since its foundation, MSV Group has developed a comprehensive understanding and expertise of executing Clean Room Solutions, Turnkey HVAC and clean room projects. MSV Group has to its credit many successful project executions extent across an array of Industrial, Commercial, Healthcare, Pharmaceutical and Biotechnology.
