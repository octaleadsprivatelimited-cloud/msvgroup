Door & Access Systems

Specialized Doors for Cleanroom & Healthcare Applications

Cleanroom-grade metallic doors and premium wooden doors designed for controlled environments, with interlock systems, vision panels and ISO-compliant sealing.

Clean Room Doors

Purpose-built stainless steel and GI door assemblies engineered to maintain pressure cascades and regulatory compliance.

  • SS304 / GI shells with PUF insulation core
  • Electromagnetic interlocks with LED status indicators
  • Flush vision panels in safety glass or acrylic
  • Perimeter neoprene gasketing for airtight seals
  • Auto-close mechanisms with door status monitoring
  • Fire-rated configurations for GMP / healthcare audits

Interlock Intelligence

Integrated electrical and hardware packages that support smooth personnel and material flow.

  • Programmable electromagnetic locks with alarm interfaces
  • Door status beacons for sterile zone monitoring
  • Integration-ready for BMS, access control & HVAC logic
Schedule a Site Audit

Wooden Doors

Premium door sets crafted for healthcare and hospitality zones without compromising on safety.

  • Hardwood frames with fire-retardant treatment
  • HPL or PVC laminate finishes for hygienic upkeep
  • Hospital-grade specifications for OT, ICU and recovery rooms
  • Compatible with access control, interlocks and vision panels

Applications & Snapshot

Versatile door programs engineered for sterile, critical care and hygienic production environments.

  • Pharmaceutical cleanrooms & manufacturing suites
  • Hospital OT, ICU and isolation rooms
  • Laboratories, research facilities & diagnostics
  • Food processing & hygienic packaging areas
