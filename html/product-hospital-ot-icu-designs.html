Hospital OT and ICU Designs

Critical Care Projects

Turnkey Hospital OT & ICU Design & Execution

Complete design and execution services for operation theaters, intensive care units and critical care areas. ISO-compliant HVAC, modular partitions, medical gas pipelines, electrical systems and specialized equipment integration.

Design Scope

End-to-end planning that aligns clinical workflows with regulatory mandates.

  • Layout planning and space optimisation
  • HVAC design: Laminar flow systems, temperature/humidity control
  • Modular wall/ceiling partitions with PUF panels
  • Medical gas pipeline system (O2, Vacuum, Compressed Air)
  • Electrical: UPS, lighting, data/communication outlets
  • Fire safety, access control and monitoring systems
  • Bed head panels, surgical lighting, pendants

Built For Compliance

Documentation and infrastructure that pass audits without disruption.

  • Careful zoning for sterile pathways and critical workflows
  • End-to-end HVAC validation with laminar flow certification
  • Integrated medical gas, UPS and safety systems
Schedule a Design Workshop

Execution Services

Coordinated project delivery that keeps operations on schedule.

  • Civil and structural modifications
  • MEP installation and commissioning
  • Equipment integration and calibration
  • Validation documentation (IQ/OQ/PQ)
  • Staff training and handover

Standards Compliance

Designed and executed to meet global hospital accreditation requirements.

  • NABH/NABL guidelines
  • ISO 14644 (cleanroom standards)
  • IS 14644 for medical gas systems
  • NBC (National Building Code)
  • BIS standards for electrical
