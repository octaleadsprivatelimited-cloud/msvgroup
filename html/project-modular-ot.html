Tri-Theater Modular OT & Recovery Block

MSV Group executed a turnkey modular OT complex for a multi-specialty hospital in Chennai, delivering precision-engineered operating theaters, sterile support rooms and a digital command backbone for surgical teams.

Chennai, Tamil Nadu 7-week program 5,400 sq.ft build

Scope Delivered

Complete OT infrastructure from civil rework to validation and handover.

  • Three ISO Class 5 operating rooms with modular walls, flush glazing and pendant booms
  • Dedicated scrub, prep, clean utility and sterile storage connected via sterile corridor
  • HVAC design featuring laminar diffusers, VAV boxes and pressure cascade logic
  • Integrated nurse call, PACS display, OT lights and integration panels
  • Post-operative recovery bay with medical gas and bed head systems

Key Systems Installed

Precision mechanical and digital systems for surgical excellence.

  • Laminar ceiling with HEPA H14 filtration and temperature humidity control
  • Advanced surgical control panels integrating HVAC, lighting and video
  • Fire detection, medical gas alarms and UPS-backed critical circuits
  • Antimicrobial vinyl floor with conductive underlay and smooth transitions

Project Outcomes

Operational agility and infection control gains for surgical teams.

  • 30% increase in daily surgeries with optimised patient flow
  • Significant reduction in airborne counts during theatre testing
  • Positive feedback from surgeons on ergonomics and digital integration

Support & Training

Ensuring continuous uptime and compliance readiness.

  • Clinical staff training on smart control panels and medical gas safety
  • Structured maintenance program for HVAC, filtration and electrical systems
  • Annual validation support for NABH and ISO 14644 audits
